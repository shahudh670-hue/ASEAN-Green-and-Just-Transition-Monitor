# ASEAN Green and Just Transition Monitor

An open, fully client-side dashboard from the United Nations Development Programme that tracks the energy transition across the eleven ASEAN members alongside the things that decide whether it is fair and financeable: human development, multidimensional poverty, fiscal space, external debt, domestic capital markets and international finance flows.

**Live site:** https://shahudh670-hue.github.io/ASEAN-Green-and-Just-Transition-Monitor/

The monitor asks one question: where can scarce public and concessional finance unlock the most private and domestic investment in a transition that also improves people's lives?

## What is inside

| Pillar | Indicators | Primary source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy transition and investment | Renewable share of final energy; carbon intensity of GDP; access to electricity; FDI net inflows; gross capital formation; international public finance for clean energy (SDG 7.a.1) | IEA, EDGAR, Tracking SDG7, IMF BoP, national accounts (via World Bank WDI); OECD and IRENA (via UN SDG Global Database) |
| Human development and planetary pressures | HDI; planetary pressures-adjusted HDI; HDI loss from planetary pressures; inequality-adjusted HDI; CO2 and material footprint per capita; 2026 global MPI | UNDP Human Development Report Office; UNDP and OPHI |
| Income level and growth | GDP per capita (PPP and constant US$); GDP per capita growth; FY2027 income group and lending category | World Bank |
| Fiscal space and domestic resource mobilisation | General government expenditure, revenue, net lending and gross debt; tax revenue | IMF Fiscal Monitor and World Economic Outlook; IMF GFS (via WDI) |
| External debt and its composition | External debt stocks; public, private non-guaranteed and short-term shares; debt service | World Bank International Debt Statistics |
| Domestic financial and capital markets | Private credit (total and from banks); stock market capitalisation; value of stocks traded | IMF IFS and World Federation of Exchanges (via WDI) |
| Official development assistance | Net ODA received (% of GNI); share of the ASEAN total | OECD DAC (via WDI) |

Every indicator, its code, unit and source link is listed in [`config/indicators.json`](config/indicators.json) and on the site's *Sources and methods* page.

## Interactive features

- Linked choropleth map and ranking: hover in one, see it in the other; click to highlight a country everywhere on the page (up to four, colour stays with the country).
- Year slider with play-through animation, regional aggregate options (GDP- or population-weighted, median, or total) and IMF projections on demand.
- Trend chart with zoom, rebasing to 100 and a full-member tooltip; a country-by-year heat grid that moves the whole page to the clicked year.
- Relationship explorer: any two indicators, bubble size by population or GDP, log scales, animated years and trails.
- Country profiles with latest values, five-year change judged against each indicator's direction, rank and sparkline.
- Automated policy insights generated by fixed, tested rules, each with the numbers used and a one-click jump to the chart behind it.
- Table view, CSV and PNG download on every chart; shareable links that preserve the full view; light and dark themes.

## How the data pipeline works

`scripts/etl/run.mjs` pulls every series directly from its official interface:

- World Bank Indicators API v2 (WDI and International Debt Statistics)
- IMF DataMapper API (World Economic Outlook and Fiscal Monitor)
- UN SDG Global Database API (SDG 7.a.1)
- UNDP HDRO composite indices time series (CSV)

The 2026 global MPI table and the FY2027 income classification are small published tables kept in `config/static/`, with their sources.

The pipeline derives the debt-composition and ODA-share series, checks results against reference values recorded when the indicator set was designed (`config/reference-checks.json`), and writes `public/data/data.json` plus a long-format CSV. If a source is unreachable, the previous values are kept and the failure is shown on the *Sources and methods* page. Nothing is imputed.

The GitHub Actions workflow in `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` runs the pipeline, the tests and the build on every push and on the 2nd of each month, then publishes to GitHub Pages.

## Run it locally

```bash
npm ci
npm run data     # fetch the data (needs internet access)
npm test         # unit tests for the insight rules
npm run dev      # local server
```
